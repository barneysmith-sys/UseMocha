About Mocha
What Mocha is

Mocha is an AI-powered interview preparation platform. You answer practice questions — behavioral, competency, and situational — and receive scored, structured feedback calibrated against the hiring standards used at top consulting, finance, and technology firms.

Mocha is an independent product. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or partnered with any firm referenced in our feedback content. Company names are used solely to describe publicly known hiring benchmarks.

Beta disclaimer

Mocha is actively being developed. Features may change, things may break, and AI feedback is coaching guidance only — not a formal assessment or guarantee of any hiring outcome.

Liability

Mocha is provided as-is, without warranties of any kind. We are not responsible for interview outcomes, job offers, or hiring decisions. Use feedback as one input among many.

Your Data
What we collect

We are transparent about every piece of data we collect and why.

  • Name & email address — required to create and identify your account. Used to send transactional emails (account confirmation, password reset, billing receipts) and, if you opt in, product updates.
  • Interview answers — the text you submit in practice sessions is sent to our AI model to generate feedback. We retain this data to improve scoring accuracy and personalise your experience over time.
  • Session & performance history — your completed sessions, scores, and streaks are stored on our servers to power your progress dashboard and to improve the product.
  • Usage analytics — page views, feature interactions, and general usage patterns collected via Google Analytics. Analytics data is pseudonymised and does not include your name or email address directly.
  • Payment data — if you subscribe to Pro, Stripe collects your payment details. We receive and store only a tokenised reference, your subscription status, and billing history. We never see your full card number.
  • Device & browser data — IP address, browser type, operating system, and approximate location (country/region level) collected automatically when you use the platform. Used for security, fraud prevention, and aggregate analytics.
How we use your data
  • To provide the service — generating AI feedback, saving your history, processing payments, and sending transactional emails.
  • To improve Mocha — analysing usage patterns and reviewing anonymised interview responses to improve scoring quality. We do not use your data to train third-party AI models without your explicit consent.
  • To keep you informed — product updates, new features, and beta announcements if you have opted in. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time via the link in any email we send.
  • For security & fraud prevention — detecting abuse, protecting accounts, and complying with legal obligations.

We never sell your data. We do not use your data for behavioural advertising or share it with advertisers. No exceptions.

Data sharing

We share data only with the sub-processors needed to operate the service:

  • Stripe — payment processing
  • Google Analytics — usage analytics
  • Anthropic / OpenAI — AI feedback generation
  • Hosting provider — infrastructure

We do not sell data to third parties, data brokers, or advertising networks.

Data retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will erase your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law (e.g. billing records for tax compliance, typically 7 years).

Anonymised, aggregated analytics data may be retained indefinitely.

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect and how it is used, to request deletion, and to opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To exercise your rights, contact us at help@usemocha.app.
