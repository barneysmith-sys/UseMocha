Everything you need to know

What makes Mocha different from other interview prep?

Most tools give you generic feedback. Mocha scores your answer on the exact four dimensions McKinsey, Goldman and Google interviewers use: Structure, Clarity, Ownership and Impact. You get a number, a breakdown of where you lost points, and a rewritten version of your answer at the standard that gets the offer. Career centres cannot do that, and a general purpose chatbot does not know what a 9.1 sounds like.

Is it actually free?

Yes. One session a day with full firm grade scoring, a STAR breakdown and a shareable score card. No credit card and no trial clock. 3,000 students have practised on Mocha. Pro is $9 a month for unlimited sessions, your university ranking, and the rewritten answer at offer standard.

How much time does it take?

About five minutes. One question, your answer, then an instant score and feedback. Nothing to book and nothing to schedule. Students use it on the commute, between classes and before bed, and 74 per cent come back the following week on their own.

I have a specific role or company in mind. Can Mocha prepare me for that?

Yes. There are eight tracks: Consulting, Banking, Tech and Product, Finance and Private Equity, Marketing, Healthcare, Nonprofit and Retail. Each one is calibrated to how those firms actually interview, so the rubric behind a McKinsey question is not the rubric behind a Goldman one.
