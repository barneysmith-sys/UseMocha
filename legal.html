About Mocha
What Mocha is

Mocha is an AI behavioral interview coach. You answer practice questions and receive scored feedback calibrated against real-world evaluation frameworks used at top consulting, finance, and tech firms.

Mocha is not affiliated with or endorsed by any firm referenced in the feedback. Those names describe publicly known hiring standards only.

Beta disclaimer

Mocha is a work in progress. Things may break or change. Feedback is AI-generated coaching guidance, not a formal assessment. Don’t treat a Mocha score as an official evaluation.

Liability

Mocha is provided as-is. We’re not responsible for interview outcomes or hiring decisions. Use the feedback as coaching input alongside your own judgment.

Your Data
What we collect

We collect as little as possible.

  • Name & email — to identify your account and send occasional product updates. You can opt out any time.
  • Interview answers — processed in real time to generate your feedback. Not stored after your session ends.
  • Usage analytics — anonymised behaviour data via Google Analytics. No name, no email.
  • Local data — your history, streak, and settings live in your browser only and never leave your device.
How we use your data

Your name and email create your account and let us send product updates.

We never sell your data. No advertisers. No profiling. No exceptions.

Your rights

Email us any time to:

  • Delete your account and all data
  • See everything we hold about you
  • Unsubscribe from product emails
Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how people use Mocha. It doesn’t collect your name or email. You can opt out via the GA opt-out add-on.

Security

All credentials are stored server-side and never exposed in frontend code. We’ll notify you promptly if anything goes wrong.
